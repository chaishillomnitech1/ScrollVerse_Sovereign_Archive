# FlameHeir Strategy

## Overview
FLAMEHEIR aims to promote global co-participation through effective outreach and collaboration with artists and creators. This document outlines our strategy and initial tasks to achieve the XLVIII SOVEREIGN co-participation goals.

## Initial Tasks for Outreach
1. Identify key community influencers in the art and creator sector.
2. Develop a targeted outreach plan that includes social media campaigns, newsletters, and direct engagement.
3. Create onboarding materials to familiarize new participants with the program's objectives and benefits.
4. Establish communication channels for ongoing support and feedback.
5. Monitor engagement metrics to adjust strategies as needed.

## Onboarding Templates
- Artist Template: Outline the specific benefits and responsibilities for artists participating in FLAMEHEIR.
- Creator Template: Tailor onboarding materials for creators, focusing on collaboration and community engagement.

## Next Steps
- Finalize onboarding templates and distribute them to initial recruits.
- Launch the outreach campaign by the end of Q1 2026.
